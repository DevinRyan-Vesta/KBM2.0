Every key, lockbox, and sign your team owns — accounted for.

VKeys is inventory management built for real estate. Track keys and their copies, rotate lockbox codes, manage signs and smart locks, and always know who has what and when it's due back.

🔑

Keys & copies

Check keys in and out by the copy, assign them to tenants, contractors, or properties, and get flagged when they're overdue.

🔒

Lockboxes & codes

Codes rotate automatically on every checkout, with the current and previous code always on record.

🪧

Signs & smart locks

Track sign pieces and assembled units, riders, condition — plus smart lock codes tied to properties.

🏠

Properties & contacts

Link every item to a property, unit, and contact, so checkout history follows the people and places involved.

🧾

Receipts & audit trail

Automatic receipts, activity logs, and inventory audits keep your records clean and defensible.

⚙️

API & integrations

A full REST API connects VKeys to your CRM, spreadsheets, or automation tools.
