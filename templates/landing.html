Purpose-built for field inventory

Not a spreadsheet, not a generic asset tracker - a system shaped around how real estate teams actually move keys, lockboxes, and signs.

Keys & copies

Check keys in and out by the copy, assign them to tenants, contractors, or properties, and get flagged the moment one is overdue.

Lockboxes & codes

Codes rotate automatically on every checkout, with the current and previous code always on record.

Signs & smart locks

Track sign pieces and assembled units, riders and condition - plus smart lock codes tied to properties.

Properties & contacts

Link every item to a property, unit, and contact, so checkout history follows the people and places involved.

Receipts & audits

Automatic checkout receipts, activity logs, and physical inventory audits keep your records clean and defensible.

API & integrations

A complete REST API connects VKeys to your CRM, spreadsheets, or automation tools - everything the app can do, your software can too.
